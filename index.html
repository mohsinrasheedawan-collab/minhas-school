Welcome

A school built on steady, honest work

Minhas Public School & College has taught the children of Mansehra for more than twenty-five years. We teach a full course from Play Group to Pre-Medical, we keep a written record for every child, and we tell parents the truth about their son or daughter's progress. Results report a percentage and a grade. We do not publish positions or rankings, because a child should be measured against the subject and not against the next desk.

About the school

Our story, our campuses

ہمارے بارے میں

Since 1998

The school opened its doors in 1998 with a small roll and one building. It now teaches about 450 students across two campuses in Mansehra, with 27 members of staff. Growth has been slow and deliberate. Every class added was added only once the teachers were in place to carry it.

What we are here to do

To give every child a complete education in a safe, orderly school, taught by qualified teachers, recorded honestly, and reported to parents in plain language. Learn, Lead, Excel is not decoration. It is the order we believe these three things come in.

How we report results

Every student sits a monthly test. Each term a Detailed Marks Certificate goes home showing subject-wise marks, the percentage and the grade, with a written remark from the teacher. We do not award positions and we do not publish rankings.

Admission is open

داخلے جاری ہیں

Who may apply

  • Play Group from age three
  • Classes 1 to 8, any time a seat is open
  • Classes 9 and 10, boys and girls sections
  • XI and XII Pre-Medical, on matriculation result

Documents to bring

  • Child's Form B or birth certificate

  • Two passport size photographs
  • School leaving certificate and last result card, for a transfer

  • Call the office or send the form below
  • Visit the campus and meet the Principal

Facilities and systems

A school that keeps its records

سہولیات اور نظام

Much of what we have built is not visible from the gate. It is the record keeping behind it that lets us answer a parent's question the same day it is asked.

QR attendance at the gate

Every student carries an ID card with a QR code. It is scanned on arrival, so the register is closed within minutes of the first bell.

Online examination portal

Monthly tests and term papers are set and marked through the school portal, which feeds straight into the result record.

WhatsApp to parents, in Urdu

Attendance and results reach parents on WhatsApp in Urdu, so no message depends on a child carrying a slip home.

Monthly fee vouchers

Each voucher carries the child's photograph and a QR code, with the last three months shown on the same slip. Payable at the office, by Easypaisa or at Bank Alfalah.

A full record for every child

Admission papers, photograph, attendance, monthly tests, term results and fee history are held in one student record from the day of admission.
